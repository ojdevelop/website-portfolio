A bit about me / 04

Curiosity first.
Player always.

I’m Oj, a game designer focused on gameplay and systems. I care about the small details that make an experience click: readable feedback, a satisfying rhythm, and the feeling that every interaction matters.

My work spans combat, progression, balancing, level flow, and production support. I use Unity and Unreal Engine, and enjoy collaborating with teams to turn an early idea into something people can play.

Areas of focus
